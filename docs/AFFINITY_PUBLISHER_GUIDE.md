# Affinity Publisher Guide

## New Document Setup

Create a new Affinity Publisher document with:

- Trim size: 8.5 x 8.5 inches
- Page count: 32 pages
- Facing pages: on
- DPI: 300
- Color format while designing: RGB/8
- Color profile: sRGB
- Bleed: 0.125 inches on all sides
- Margins: 0.5 inches on all sides

## Illustration Placement

- Place illustrations full bleed.
- Full bleed art should cover 8.75 x 8.75 inches.
- Keep important faces, hands, and story action inside the 0.5 inch safe margin.
- Do not bake story text into images.
- Add story text as editable Affinity Publisher text boxes.

## Text Handling

- Use paragraph styles for story text.
- Keep text readable against the illustration.
- Avoid placing text over busy details.
- Keep title, story copy, and activity text editable until final export.

## Export

Export the final book as PDF/X-4 with:

- Bleed included
- Fonts embedded
- Correct page order
- No printer marks unless Lulu specifically requests them
- Final proof reviewed before approval

Keep Lulu exports in `05_Print/Lulu/` or `data/exports/` depending on whether the file is a final submission artifact or a local app export.
