# Lulu Print Guide

## Required Specs

- Trim size: 8.5 x 8.5 inches
- Bleed: 0.125 inches
- Full bleed art size: 8.75 x 8.75 inches
- Required pixels at 300 DPI: 2625 x 2625 px
- Safe margin: 0.5 inches

## Before Upload

- Confirm all 32 pages are present.
- Confirm page order.
- Confirm images extend to bleed where needed.
- Confirm story text stays inside safe margins.
- Confirm no image contains baked-in story text.
- Confirm fonts are embedded in the exported PDF.
- Confirm cover dimensions with Lulu's cover calculator.

## Proofing

Order a printed proof before release. Record notes in:

```text
05_Print/Proofs/
```

Track fixes before uploading a final replacement PDF.
