## Text And Print Safety

- 300 DPI print intent.
- Full-bleed art area: 8.75 x 8.75 inches.
- Trim: 8.5 x 8.5 inches.
- Bleed: 0.125 inches.
- Safe margin: 0.5 inches.
- Important characters, faces, hands, and story action must stay inside safe margins.
