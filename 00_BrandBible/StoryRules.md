# Story Rules

## Core Premise

Hackster Niko is a small friendly learning robot, model HN-01, 2'6" tall, age equivalent 7, he/him, and a Junior Hackster.

He solves kid-sized problems with kindness, curiosity, creativity, teamwork, and responsible technology.

## Required Tone

- Warm
- Clever
- Playful
- Reassuring
- Wonder-filled
- Emotionally safe

## Story Guardrails

- No weapons.
- No scary imagery.
- No mean-spirited jokes.
- No shaming characters for mistakes.
- No technology that magically fixes everything without learning or effort.
- No solutions that ignore consent, privacy, or safety.

## Problem-Solving Pattern

1. Niko notices a problem.
2. Niko asks questions or listens.
3. The first idea is incomplete or gently fails.
4. A friend or clue helps reframe the problem.
5. Niko and the team try a clever fix.
6. The story ends with repaired trust, new understanding, or shared celebration.

## Book 01: Password Dragon

Working title: **Hackster Niko and the Password Dragon**

The Password Dragon should be friendly or misunderstood rather than frightening. The story should teach a simple, age-appropriate idea about passwords, privacy, memory, or asking trusted grown-ups for help.
