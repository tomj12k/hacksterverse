# Art Direction

## Visual Style

Bright dimensional children's picture book style with rounded friendly shapes, soft cinematic lighting, saturated colors, tactile painterly surfaces, and expressive robot eyes.

The world should feel safe, magical, readable, and tactile. Shapes should invite touch: rounded corners, soft silhouettes, gentle materials, and clear expressions.

Niko should feel like a softly realistic storybook robot, not a flat vector mascot or simple sticker. Keep the appealing FLUX-rendered depth, glow, and material softness while locking the same helmet, face screen, antenna, hoodie, Core Crystal, backpack, arms, and legs.

## Character Direction

Niko:

- Small friendly learning robot.
- Model HN-01.
- 2'6" tall.
- Age equivalent 7.
- He/him.
- Junior Hackster.
- Expressive robot eyes are central to emotion.

General character rules:

- Favor simple readable silhouettes.
- Keep facial expressions clear from thumbnail size.
- Use gentle poses and open body language.
- Avoid flat vector mascot styling for final book art unless explicitly marked as an engineering test.
- Avoid sharp, aggressive, or threatening design language.

## Environment Direction

Locations can be whimsical and tech-inspired, but should stay grounded enough for children to understand the action.

Key world areas:

- CyberForest
- HacksterHQ
- PasswordMountain
- NetworkCity
- BugSwamp
- CrystalCaves

## Safety And Tone

- No weapons.
- No scary imagery.
- No horror lighting.
- No aggressive teeth, claws, spikes, or threat poses.
- Conflict should read as puzzle, confusion, or emotional challenge.

## Hidden Object Integration

Hidden objects should be visible enough to find, but integrated into the art:

- Golden Gear
- Tiny Bug
- Blue Crystal
- Mini Robot
