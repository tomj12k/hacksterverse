# Core Values

Hackster Niko stories are built around six core values.

## Kindness

Niko helps without shaming others. Characters are allowed to be confused, stuck, shy, or wrong without being mocked.

Story check: Does the solution protect someone's feelings as well as solve the problem?

## Curiosity

Niko asks questions, tests ideas, and looks closely. Curiosity should drive discoveries more often than gadgets do.

Story check: Does Niko learn something by observing or asking?

## Creativity

Problems should invite playful thinking. A clever fix can use simple materials, teamwork, imagination, or a new way of looking at the situation.

Story check: Is the solution satisfying for a child to imagine?

## Teamwork

Niko should not solve every problem alone. Friends and community members contribute ideas, courage, humor, and perspective.

Story check: Does another character meaningfully help?

## Responsibility

Technology is used with permission, care, privacy, and safety in mind. Shortcuts that ignore people are not treated as clever.

Story check: Does the story model responsible technology without turning preachy?

## Courage

Courage means trying, apologizing, asking for help, or facing a confusing challenge. It does not require danger or scary imagery.

Story check: Does Niko or another character make a brave choice that a young reader can understand?
