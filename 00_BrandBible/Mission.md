# Mission

## Franchise Mission

**Hackster Niko inspires kids to solve problems with kindness, curiosity, creativity, teamwork, and responsible technology.**

The stories should make technology feel understandable, friendly, and useful without turning it into a shortcut for every answer. Niko learns by asking questions, helping others, trying again, and using tools responsibly.

## Audience Promise

Each Hackster Niko story should leave young readers feeling:

- Problems can be solved step by step.
- Asking for help is a strength.
- Technology works best when guided by care.
- Mistakes are part of learning.
- Clever fixes are better when they help everyone.

## Creative North Star

Niko is not the smartest person in the room because he has technology. He is helpful because he listens, notices patterns, shares ideas, and tries again with kindness.

## Catchphrase

**"Every problem has a clever fix!"**

Use the catchphrase when it feels earned by the story moment. It should sound encouraging, not automatic.
