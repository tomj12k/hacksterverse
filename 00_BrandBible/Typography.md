# Typography

## Typography Goals

Typography should feel friendly, readable, and calm. The book is for children and read-aloud moments, so clarity matters more than novelty.

## Recommended Type Direction

- Rounded sans serif for headers and display moments.
- Highly readable serif or humanist sans serif for body text, depending on final art style.
- Large, open counters and generous line spacing.
- Avoid overly thin strokes, cramped letters, and novelty fonts.

## Usage Guidelines

- Keep body text away from busy illustration areas.
- Maintain consistent text placement patterns across spreads.
- Use strong contrast between text and background.
- Keep line lengths short enough for young readers.
- Avoid placing important text inside trim or bleed areas.

## Production Notes

- Record font names, versions, licenses, and source URLs before final production.
- Package fonts or outline text only according to each license and print requirement.
- Test exports from Affinity Publisher before proof submission.
